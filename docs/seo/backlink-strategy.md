# Backlink strategy

The goal is 25 landlords on guaranteed rent, not a domain-authority score.
Every tactic below is chosen because the link would also send a landlord or
investor who might use the site, and because it can be done by one person in
a few hours a week. Nothing here involves buying links, link exchanges or
private blog networks — those are the fastest way to get a new domain
ignored or penalised.

## What the site already has that people link to

Most sites start a link campaign with nothing to point at. This one has:

| Asset | Why it earns links | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 23 free calculators, no sign-up | Forum answers and blog posts cite a calculator far more readily than a company page | `/calculators/` |
| Embeddable calculators | Every embed on another site carries a link back — the one tactic here that compounds on its own | `EmbedCode` on each calculator |
| 19 free legal templates | "Free AST / Section 8 / inventory template" is a perennial search and a perennial forum question | `/templates/` |
| Top 20 BTL postcodes by yield, with sources | Data with the workings shown is what local journalists quote | `/resources/top-20-btl-postcodes/` |
| Sold prices by postcode district | Same — 41 pages a local reporter can cite by area | `/areas/postcodes/` |
| Landlord database / Ombudsman explainer | Timely: registration opens 15 December 2026 and every landlord site will be writing about it | `/landlords/prs-database/` |

## The plan, in order

### 1. Fix what makes links worthless (week 1)

Verify Search Console (see `search-console.md`). Without it you cannot see
which links Google has found, so nothing below can be measured.

### 2. Citations — the boring links that establish a real business (week 1–2)

Guaranteed rent is a local service in six Midlands cities. Local ranking
depends on consistent name, address and phone across:

- Google Business Profile (category: *Property management company*; service
  areas Birmingham, Nottingham, Derby, Leicester, Coventry, Sheffield)
- Bing Places (imports from Google Business Profile)
- Yell, Thomson Local, 192.com, Scoot, FreeIndex
- Companies House already exists — make sure the trading name and website
  there match the site exactly

One afternoon. These are low-value individually and essential collectively.

### 3. Embeds — the tactic that scales without you (ongoing)

Every calculator has an embed code. Offer it deliberately:

- Add a short "Embed this calculator on your site — free, with a credit link"
  line to the calculator pages (the component exists; the offer is not
  visible enough).
- Email letting agents, mortgage brokers and property accountants in the six
  cities: *"You can put our stamp duty / rental yield calculator on your site
  for free; it keeps your visitors on your page."* A broker's site with a
  working calculator is a better site, so the pitch is genuine.
- Target: ten embeds in ninety days. Each is a followed link from a relevant
  UK property domain.

### 4. Forum presence — answers, not adverts (30 minutes, three times a week)

Property Tribes, the Property Hub forum, LandlordZONE forums, and the
landlord threads on r/UKPersonalFinance and r/HousingUK. Answer the actual
question; link a calculator or template only when it is the answer. Most of
these links are nofollow. They still send the right visitors, and the people
who run property blogs read these forums.

Rules: never post the same link twice in a week; never mention guaranteed
rent unless someone asks about voids or management.

### 5. Digital PR — one data story a month (from week 3)

Local press will print a story built on numbers with sources. The site has
the numbers. Format each as one short email with the headline finding, a
table, and a link to the page that shows the workings:

- *"The Nottingham postcodes where buy-to-let yields 8%+"* — Nottingham Post,
  Nottinghamshire Live
- Same for Birmingham Mail, Derby Telegraph, Leicester Mercury, Coventry
  Telegraph, Sheffield Star
- Trade press for the national angle: Property Industry Eye, Landlord Today,
  LandlordZONE, Property118, Letting Agent Today, The Negotiator

The landlord database story writes itself in November: *"What Midlands
landlords need ready before 15 December"* with the fee, the fields and the
penalty tiers from the page.

Expect roughly one placement in ten pitches. Each placement is a followed
link from a domain Google trusts.

### 6. Expert quotes (10 minutes a day)

Journalists ask for landlord and property sources daily. Register on
Featured.com and Qwoted, and search `#journorequest landlord` on X each
morning. Answer in three sentences with a specific number. A quote is
usually a link.

### 7. Partners (from month 2)

The rent-to-rent and guaranteed-rent pages already say the honest thing: it
is not for every landlord. Solicitors, brokers and accountants who work with
landlords have "useful resources" pages. Offer them the templates and
calculators for their clients — with the consent-to-let page for brokers
specifically, since it answers a question they get weekly.

### 8. Instagram (already running)

Reels do not pass link equity. They do create brand searches, and Google
treats a site people search for by name as one worth ranking. Keep the
link-in-bio pointed at whichever page the Reel is about, not the homepage.

## What to measure

Monthly, from Search Console → Links and Bing Webmaster → Backlinks (free;
Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is also free for a verified site):

- Referring domains (the count that matters; not total links)
- Which pages earn links — do more of that kind
- Impressions and clicks for the six `guaranteed-rent/<city>` pages

## What not to do

- Do not buy links, join link exchanges, or post on "write for us" farms.
- Do not put the same anchor text everywhere; let people link naturally.
- Do not chase Domain Authority. It is a third-party estimate, not a Google metric.
- Do not link-build to the "coming soon" pages; they are noindexed until real.
