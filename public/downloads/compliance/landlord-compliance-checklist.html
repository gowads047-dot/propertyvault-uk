Penalties for non-compliance:
  • No deposit protection: Cannot serve Section 21; penalty of 1-3x deposit amount
  • No Gas Safety Certificate: Unlimited fine and/or up to 6 months imprisonment
  • No EPC: Fixed penalty of £200
  • No EICR: Fine up to £30,000
  • No Right to Rent check: Fine up to £20,000 per tenant and/or up to 5 years imprisonment
  • Unlicensed HMO: Unlimited fine; tenants can claim up to 12 months' rent back
