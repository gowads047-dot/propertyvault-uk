5. GUARANTEE

In consideration of the Landlord entering into the tenancy agreement with the Tenant, the Guarantor hereby guarantees to the Landlord:

  1. That the Tenant will pay the rent and all other sums due under the tenancy agreement when they fall due.
  2. That the Tenant will comply with all obligations under the tenancy agreement.
  3. That if the Tenant fails to pay any rent or other sum due, or breaches any obligation, the Guarantor will pay or make good the loss to the Landlord on demand.
  4. This guarantee is a continuing guarantee and will remain in force for the duration of the tenancy, including any statutory periodic tenancy that arises after the fixed term.
  5. The Guarantor's liability shall not exceed the equivalent of months' rent plus reasonable costs.
  6. The Landlord is not required to take action against the Tenant before making a claim against the Guarantor.
  7. This guarantee shall not be discharged or affected by any variation of the tenancy agreement, any extension of time given to the Tenant, or any other act or omission which would otherwise discharge the Guarantor.

6. INDEPENDENT LEGAL ADVICE

The Guarantor confirms that:

  • I have been advised to take independent legal advice before signing this guarantee
  • I understand the nature and extent of my obligations under this guarantee
  • I have had the opportunity to read and understand the tenancy agreement
  • I enter into this guarantee voluntarily and without undue pressure
