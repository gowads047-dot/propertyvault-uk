7. JOINT TENANT PROVISIONS

  • All tenants are jointly and severally liable for all obligations under this agreement
  • Any breach by one tenant is treated as a breach by all tenants
  • Notice to quit given by one joint tenant ends the tenancy for all joint tenants (Hammersmith v Monk [1992])
  • One joint tenant cannot be replaced without the Landlord's written consent and a new agreement
  • The deposit will be returned to one nominated tenant unless all tenants agree in writing to an alternative arrangement
