5. DEPOSIT

Deposit amount:£
Deposit protection scheme:

The deposit is held as security for the performance of the Tenant's obligations and to cover any damage, unpaid rent, or breach of this agreement. The deposit will be protected in a government-approved tenancy deposit scheme within 30 days of receipt, in accordance with the Housing Act 2004. The prescribed information about the deposit scheme will be provided to the Tenant within the same 30-day period.

The Landlord may make deductions from the deposit for:

  • Any damage to the property or its contents (beyond fair wear and tear)
  • Any unpaid rent or other charges due under this agreement
  • Any costs incurred due to the Tenant's breach of this agreement
  • Any cleaning required to return the property to the standard it was in at the start of the tenancy (as recorded in the inventory)
  • Any missing items

6. LANDLORD'S OBLIGATIONS

The Landlord agrees to:

  1. Provide the property in a good state of repair and in a condition fit for habitation at the start of the tenancy.
  2. Keep in repair the structure and exterior of the property (including drains, gutters, and external pipes) in accordance with Section 11 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985.
  3. Keep in repair and proper working order the installations for the supply of water, gas, electricity, sanitation, and for space and water heating.
  4. Ensure all gas appliances and flues are checked annually by a Gas Safe registered engineer and provide a copy of the Gas Safety Certificate (CP12) to the Tenant within 28 days of the check.
  5. Ensure the electrical installation is inspected and tested at least every 5 years and provide a copy of the Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) to the Tenant.
  6. Provide an Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) with a minimum rating of E (or such higher minimum as required by law).
  7. Ensure working smoke alarms are fitted on each storey and carbon monoxide alarms in rooms with fixed combustion appliances, in accordance with the Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (England) Regulations 2015 (as amended).
  8. Protect the deposit in a government-approved scheme and provide the prescribed information within 30 days.
  9. Provide the Tenant with a copy of the current government "How to Rent" guide before the tenancy begins.
  10. Give the Tenant at least 24 hours' written notice before entering the property (except in an emergency), and visit only at reasonable times.
  11. Carry out Right to Rent checks on all adult occupants in accordance with the Immigration Act 2014.
  12. Deal with repairs promptly and within a reasonable timescale.
  13. Insure the building (not the Tenant's contents).
  14. Provide the Tenant's deposit prescribed information, including details of the deposit protection scheme, within 30 days of receiving the deposit.

7. TENANT'S OBLIGATIONS

The Tenant agrees to:

  1. Pay the rent on time and in full on the date it is due each month.
  2. Pay all council tax, water charges, gas, electricity, telephone, internet, and television licence charges for the property during the tenancy (unless otherwise stated in this agreement).
  3. Keep the property clean, tidy, and in good condition.
  4. Not cause or permit any damage to the property or its contents beyond fair wear and tear.
  5. Report any disrepair or defect to the Landlord or Agent promptly in writing.
  6. Not make any alterations or additions to the property without the prior written consent of the Landlord.
  7. Not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the property or any part of it without the prior written consent of the Landlord.
  8. Not use the property for any illegal or immoral purpose.
  9. Not cause nuisance, annoyance, or disturbance to neighbours or other occupants of adjoining properties.
  10. Allow the Landlord or Agent access to the property to inspect its condition or carry out repairs, subject to at least 24 hours' written notice (except in an emergency).
  11. Not keep any pets at the property without the prior written consent of the Landlord. (Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.)
  12. Not smoke inside the property.
  13. Test smoke alarms and carbon monoxide detectors regularly and report any faults to the Landlord.
  14. Not remove any of the Landlord's fixtures, fittings, or contents from the property.
  15. Return the property at the end of the tenancy in the same condition as at the start (as recorded in the inventory), allowing for fair wear and tear.
  16. Provide forwarding address details to the Landlord at the end of the tenancy.
  17. Return all keys to the Landlord at the end of the tenancy.

8. ENDING THE TENANCY

During the fixed term

Neither party may end the tenancy during the fixed term unless:

  • Both parties agree in writing; or
  • A break clause is exercised (see below, if applicable); or
  • The Landlord obtains a court order under Section 8 of the Housing Act 1988 on one of the statutory grounds.

After the fixed term

Once the fixed term has ended and the tenancy has become periodic:

  • The Tenant may end the tenancy by giving at least one month's written notice, expiring on the last day of a period of the tenancy.
  • The Landlord may end the tenancy by serving a Section 21 notice giving at least two months' notice, or a Section 8 notice on the relevant grounds with the required notice period.

9. UTILITIES AND COUNCIL TAX

The Tenant is responsible for the following (unless otherwise agreed in writing):

  • Council tax
  • Water and sewerage charges
  • Gas
  • Electricity
  • Telephone / broadband
  • Television licence

The Tenant must notify the relevant utility providers and the local authority of their liability from the tenancy start date and again at the end of the tenancy.

10. INVENTORY

An inventory and schedule of condition will be prepared at the start of the tenancy and agreed by both parties. This will be used to assess the condition of the property at check-out and will form the basis for any deposit deductions. Any disputes about the inventory will be referred to the relevant deposit protection scheme's dispute resolution service.

11. DATA PROTECTION

The Landlord will process the Tenant's personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Personal data will be used for the purposes of managing the tenancy, complying with legal obligations, and protecting the Landlord's legitimate interests. Data may be shared with the deposit protection scheme, referencing agencies, and relevant authorities as required by law.

12. NOTICES

Any notices under this agreement must be given in writing and may be served by:

  • Hand delivery to the party's address
  • First class post (deemed received 2 working days after posting)
  • Email to the address specified in this agreement

13. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
