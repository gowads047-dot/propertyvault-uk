RIGHT TO RENT CHECK — RECORD
Immigration Act 2014 — England Only
Legal Requirement: All landlords in England must check that tenants and adult occupants have the right to rent before the tenancy starts. Failure to check can result in a civil penalty of up to £20,000 per occupant and/or criminal prosecution (up to 5 years imprisonment for repeat offences).

Step 1: Obtain Original Documents

You must see original documents (not copies). Accept documents from List A (unlimited right) or List B (time-limited right).

List A — Unlimited Right to Rent (one document required)

  • UK passport (current or expired)
  • Irish passport or Irish passport card
  • EU/EEA/Swiss passport or national ID card with EUSS settled status
  • Certificate of registration or naturalisation as a British citizen
  • Permanent residence card or document

List B — Time-Limited Right to Rent (follow-up check required)

  • Current passport with valid visa/leave to remain
  • Biometric Residence Permit (BRP)
  • EUSS pre-settled status (check via Home Office online service)
  • Home Office positive verification notice

Step 2: Check the Documents

  • Photos match the person
  • Dates of birth are consistent across documents
  • Documents are genuine (no signs of tampering)
  • Visa/leave has not expired
  • Name matches (or explain any difference)

Step 3: Keep Records

  • Clear copy of documents taken and stored
  • Date of check recorded on copy
  • Records to be kept for duration of tenancy + 1 year
