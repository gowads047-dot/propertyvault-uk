NOTICE REQUIRING POSSESSION OF A PROPERTY LET ON AN ASSURED SHORTHOLD TENANCY
Prescribed Form 6A — The Assured Shorthold Tenancy Notices and Prescribed Requirements (England) Regulations 2015
WARNING TO LANDLORD: This notice is only valid if ALL of the following conditions are met:
  • The tenancy deposit has been protected in a government-approved scheme AND prescribed information served
  • A current Gas Safety Certificate has been provided to the tenant
  • A valid Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) has been provided to the tenant
  • The current government "How to Rent" guide has been provided to the tenant
  • A valid EICR has been provided to the tenant (from 1 April 2021)
  • The property is not subject to an improvement notice or emergency remedial action
  • At least 4 months have elapsed since the tenancy began
  • This notice is not served within 6 months of a relevant complaint to the local authority (retaliatory eviction protection)

NOTES FOR THE TENANT:
  • This notice is the first step in the process to regain possession of the property. It does not mean you have to leave on the date specified.
  • If you do not leave the property by the date specified, the Landlord must apply to court for a possession order before you are required to leave.
  • You should seek independent legal advice immediately. You may be eligible for free legal advice from:
    • Citizens Advice — citizensadvice.org.uk
    • Shelter — shelter.org.uk — 0808 800 4444
    • Your local council's housing department
  • If you believe this notice is invalid or has been served in retaliation for a complaint, seek legal advice.
  • This notice is valid for 10 months from the date specified above. If the Landlord does not apply to court within this period, a new notice must be served.
