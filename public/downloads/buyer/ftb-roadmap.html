FIRST-TIME BUYER ROADMAP
A step-by-step guide to buying your first home in England or Wales
About this guide: This roadmap is based on UK Government guidance and property industry best practice. It provides a general overview of the home buying process. Every purchase is different — always seek advice from a qualified mortgage broker, solicitor, and surveyor.

STAGE 1: PREPARATION

✓StepNotes / Date
Check your credit score (Experian, Equifax, or TransUnion — free via ClearScore, Credit Karma)
Register on the electoral roll at your current address
Start saving for a deposit (aim for 5-15% of purchase price)
Open a Lifetime ISA (if under 40, property under £450k — 25% government bonus)
Calculate your total budget including all purchase costs (use Budget Planner template)
Research government schemes (Shared Ownership, First Homes, Right to Buy)

STAGE 2: MORTGAGE

✓StepNotes / Date
Speak to a mortgage broker or bank about borrowing capacity
Get a Mortgage Agreement in Principle (AIP / DIP)
Gather documents: payslips (3 months), bank statements (3 months), ID, proof of deposit

STAGE 3: PROPERTY SEARCH

✓StepNotes / Date
Register on Rightmove, Zoopla, and OnTheMarket
Register with local estate agents
Set up property alerts for your criteria
Attend viewings (use Property Viewing Checklist)
Research the area (schools, crime, transport, flood risk)

STAGE 4: MAKING AN OFFER

✓StepNotes / Date
Research comparable sold prices (Land Registry, Rightmove)
Make your offer through the estate agent
Negotiate if necessary
Offer accepted — get confirmation in writing

STAGE 5: LEGAL PROCESS

✓StepNotes / Date
Instruct a solicitor or conveyancer
Submit full mortgage application
Commission a survey (Level 2 Homebuyer or Level 3 Building Survey)
Solicitor conducts searches (local authority, environmental, water)
Solicitor raises enquiries with seller's solicitor
Receive and review mortgage offer
Review contract pack and report on title from solicitor
Arrange buildings insurance (required from exchange)

STAGE 6: EXCHANGE & COMPLETION

✓StepNotes / Date
Sign contract and transfer deposit to solicitor
Exchange contracts (you are now legally committed)
Arrange removals
Set up utility accounts and council tax
Completion day — keys handed over, you own your home!
Register with GP, dentist, and update your address everywhere
